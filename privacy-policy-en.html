1. Introduction

Rise Against Hunger Italia ("we", "Controller") respects the privacy of its users. This notice, provided under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), explains which personal data we process through the Rise Against Hunger Italia mobile application («RAH Italia» on your phone), why we process it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

2. What data we process

The app can be used without signing up: its information content (impact figures, projects, who we are) is visible to everyone. If you choose to create an account, with email and password, we process the following data.

Category Data When
Account data Email address and password (the latter stored only in irreversibly encrypted form) Sign-up
Profile data First name, last name, nickname (optional), phone, city, province, country, date of birth (used to check the minimum age of 14), contact email Sign-up and profile changes

The app does not access your device's location, does not show ads, does not use advertising identifiers, does not use analytics or behavioural tracking tools and does not automatically send error reports to external services. You can choose to send us a report as described below. Your sign-in session is stored only on your device, in a protected area; some preferences (for example whether you have already seen a notice) also stay only on your device.

If you installed the app from Google Play, Google may collect performance and crash statistics according to your device settings and its own privacy policy; we only receive them in aggregate form.

2.1 Problem reports

«Report a problem» prepares an email only if you choose to tap it. You can read and edit the text before sending it, or cancel. If no email app opens, «Share the text» lets you pick another app and the recipient. Nothing is sent automatically and you can use the app without ever reporting a problem.

The diagnostics contain the app version and update, runtime, channel and whether it was an emergency launch; operating system and version, device manufacturer and model; language, text scale and window size. They include at most the last 10 errors: UTC time, app branch, error name and code, status and key of the translated message, filtered to exclude personal data. They do not include raw error messages, stack traces, account identifier, email, phone number, tokens, URLs, serial number or device fingerprint. The errors come from the app's temporary in-memory buffer: it is not a persistent archive and is lost when the app restarts.

If you send the email, Rise Against Hunger Italia receives the diagnostics, the text you add and your sender address at info@riseagainsthunger.it, a mailbox run on Google Workspace. Please do not add passwords, sensitive data or other unnecessary data. The report is used to answer your request and fix the problem; it is not automatically linked to your profile in the app. The email service involves transfers outside the EU, including to the United States; see section 5. TLS for the email depends on the sender's email provider and cannot be guaranteed by the app.

Legal basis: to be validated — the consultant must define the legal basis under Art. 6 GDPR for handling reports.

Retention: the email stays in the support mailbox even if you delete your app account; you can ask us to delete it by writing to us. to be validated — the consultant must set how long reports are kept and the deletion criteria.

4. Recipients and data processors

Data is processed through providers acting on our behalf:

  • Google Workspace — runs the info@riseagainsthunger.it mailbox that receives voluntary problem reports (section 2.1).
  • Supabase Inc. — database and authentication. Data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Confirm that the DPA with standard contractual clauses is signed.
  • Expo (650 Industries Inc., USA) — delivery of app updates (see section 8).
  • Service email provider — sending technical emails (sign-up confirmation, password recovery). Today this is Supabase's built-in service; before the public launch we will move to a dedicated provider (planned: Resend, Resend Inc., USA). Update this entry and the DPA when the dedicated provider is active.

When you leave the app for our partners' platforms (donations, shop, events), some data is sent to companies that are not our providers: we qualify our relationship with them as independent controllers (qualification pending legal confirmation — see section 9). Section 9 lists exactly what is sent and to whom.

Data is not sold or transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Your account and profile data are stored in the European Union. The following leave the EU, towards the United States:

  • problem reports you choose to send, the email text and the sender address, through Google Workspace;
  • the installation identifier and IP address, to Expo, to download app updates;
  • the email address we send service emails to, to the email provider once it is the dedicated one (Resend);
  • first name, last name and email, to Donorbox, only if you choose to prefill the donation form (section 9).

For these transfers we rely on appropriate safeguards under Chapter V of the GDPR (standard contractual clauses and, where available, certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Confirm the applicable safeguard for each provider.

6. Data retention

  • Account and profile data: as long as the account is active. You can delete your account directly in the app: deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Consent record: deleted together with the account. If the lawyer considers it necessary to keep it as proof of consent, the database must be changed and this entry updated.
  • Referral code: after the account is deleted we keep a copy unlinked from your identity, so we can ask partners who received it to delete what they linked to it. On its own it cannot identify you; it currently has no automatic expiry. Set a retention period.
  • Problem reports: they stay in the support mailbox after the account is deleted. How long and the deletion criteria are to be validated, as stated in section 2.1.
  • Technical logs: automatically deleted by the provider within 7 days.
  • Installation identifier: stays on your device as long as the app is installed; at Expo according to the provider's retention. Check Expo's retention periods.

7. Your rights (Arts. 15-22 GDPR)

You have the right to access your data, to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You can withdraw your marketing consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Directly in the app you can export your data in a readable format (account, profile and consent history) and delete your account (Profile → Privacy and data → Delete account → Delete now). If you no longer have the app, the Delete your account page explains how to request it by email. For any other request, write to the contacts in section 12: we will reply within one month.

If you believe the processing infringes the GDPR, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (www.garanteprivacy.it).

8. Over-The-Air (OTA) updates

The app uses the Expo Updates service (650 Industries Inc., USA) to receive updates to its code. Every time it starts it checks for an update and sends: a random identifier generated at installation and stored on the device, the app version and the platform, plus the IP address needed for the connection. It sends no account data; the identifier changes if you reinstall the app.

Money donations and charity purchases take place outside the app, on partner platforms that we qualify as independent controllers (qualification pending legal confirmation: their privacy notice says so, but a unilateral statement is not an agreement between the two companies):

  • Donorbox (Rebel Idealist LLC, USA) — donation page. The data you enter in the form (name, email, payment data) is collected by Donorbox under its privacy notice.
  • Let's Donation (Let's Donation S.r.l., Bologna) — charity shop, gift cards, events and projects, with an optional separate sign-up on their platform, governed by their privacy notice. Qualify the relationship (independent controllers vs joint controllership Art. 26 vs processor Art. 28) with the lawyer.

What we send when you open one of these links

When you tap one of these buttons, the app takes you to your device's browser and adds some information to the web address. In detail:

  • To Donorbox, if you have an account with a complete profile and have accepted the current privacy notice: first name, last name and email address from your profile, so you don't have to type again the data you already gave us, together with the referral code. The first time we ask whether you want to send them or continue without your data, and we remember your choice. Without an account, with an incomplete profile or if you choose to continue without, we send neither your name, nor your last name, nor your email.
  • To Let's Donation: only the referral code. No personal details. The first time we show you a screen telling you where you are going.

The referral code is a random code we generate: it contains neither your name nor your email and gives access to nothing. It is still data about you, because we can link it back to your account. You can ask us to cancel it by writing to the contacts at the bottom of this page.

Since the link opens in your device's browser, this information appears in the web address and stays in the browser history.

10. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures: encryption of data in transit and at rest, access rules so that each user can only read and change their own data (row-level security), passwords stored only in irreversibly encrypted form, and the session kept in the device's secure storage.

11. Minors and automated decision-making

To create an account you must be at least 14 years old: your date of birth is used to check this. The app does not use automated decision-making or profiling.
