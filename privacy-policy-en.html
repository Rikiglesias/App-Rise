1. Introduction

Rise Against Hunger Italia ("we", "Controller") respects the privacy of its users. This notice, provided under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR"), explains which personal data we process through the Rise Against Hunger Italia mobile application, why we process it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

2. What data we process

The app can be browsed without registration: informational content (projects, figures, news) is visible to everyone. If you choose to create an account for the donor area, we process the following data.

The app does not access the device's GPS location, shows no advertising, uses no advertising identifiers and runs no commercial analytics or behavioural tracking. Your sign-in session is stored only on your device, in a protected area.

4. Recipients and data processors

Data is processed through providers acting on our behalf:

  • Supabase Inc. — database and authentication. Data resides on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Confirm signature of the DPA with standard contractual clauses.
  • Functional Software Inc. (Sentry) — collection of error reports. Verify the data ingestion region (EU or US) and the DPA.
  • Expo (650 Industries Inc.) — distribution of app updates (see section 8).
  • Service email provider — sending technical emails (registration confirmation, password recovery). Currently Supabase's built-in service; if switching to a dedicated provider (e.g. Resend), update this entry and the DPA.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, those companies process the sign-in data as independent controllers, under their own privacy notices.

When you leave the app for our partners' platforms (donations, shop, events) some data is transmitted to companies acting as independent controllers, not as our providers: you will find exactly what is transmitted, and to whom, in section 9.

Data is neither sold nor transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.

6. Data retention

  • Account and profile data: for as long as the account remains active. You can delete your account directly from the app, choosing between immediate deletion or deletion scheduled in 30 days (a grace period during which you can cancel the request); once it expires, profile data is permanently erased.
  • Consent log: kept after account deletion as evidence of the given consent, for the time needed for defence against claims. Define the exact duration with legal counsel (typically up to 10 years).
  • Error reports: kept for the diagnostics provider's retention period (90 days by default). Confirm the retention configured on Sentry.

7. Your rights (Arts. 15-22 GDPR)

You have the right to access your data, to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

Directly from the app you can: export your data in a readable format (profile and consent history) and delete your account (immediately, or as a scheduled request in 30 days, see section 6). For any other request write to the contacts in section 12: we will reply within 30 days.

If you believe the processing infringes the GDPR, you may lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority, Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

8. Over-The-Air (OTA) updates

The app uses the Expo Updates service to receive updates to its own code. The update request transmits only technical information about the installed app version, with no data from your account.

9. Donations, shop and events on external platforms

Money donations and solidarity purchases happen outside the app, on partner platforms acting as independent controllers:

  • Donorbox (Rebel Idealist LLC, USA) — donation page. The data you enter in the form (name, email, payment data) is collected by Donorbox under its own privacy notice.
  • Let's Donation (Let's Donation S.r.l., Bologna, Italy) — solidarity shop, gift cards, events and projects, with a separate registration on their platform, governed by their privacy notice. Qualify the relationship (independent controllers vs joint controllership Art. 26 vs processor Art. 28) with legal counsel.

What we transmit when you open one of these links

When you tap one of these buttons, the app takes you to your device browser and adds some information to the web address. Specifically:

  • To Donorbox, if you are signed in: the first name, last name and email address from your profile, so you do not have to type again what you already gave us, together with a source reference code that lets us know the donation comes from the app. If you are not signed in, we transmit nothing.
  • To Let's Donation: the source reference code only. No personal details.

The source reference code is a random string generated by us: it contains neither your name nor your email, and it does not grant access to anything. It still is data relating to you, because we can link it back to your account. You may ask us to revoke it by writing to the contacts at the bottom of this page.

Because the link opens in your device browser, this information appears in the web address and remains in your browser history. Before you leave the app we show you a screen telling you where you are going.

10. Security

We adopt appropriate technical and organisational measures: encryption of data in transit and at rest, access rules under which each user can read and modify only their own data (row-level security), passwords stored exclusively in irreversibly encrypted form, session kept in the device's secure storage.

11. Minors and automated decision-making

The donor area is restricted to adults: the date of birth is used to verify this. The app uses no automated decision-making or profiling.
