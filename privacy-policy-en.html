Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2025

1. Introduction

Rise Against Hunger Italia ("we", "our") respects the privacy of our users ("user" or "you"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Rise Against Hunger Italia mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

When you use the Rise Against Hunger Italia application, we may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Authentication data: credentials necessary for account access and user session maintenance
  • User preferences: settings and customizations related to application usage

Data we DO NOT collect:

  • Real-time geolocation data
  • Information for advertising profiling purposes
  • User activity outside the application

3. Purpose of Processing and Legal Basis

Personal data collected is processed for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: delivery of application features (legal basis: contract execution)
  • Service Improvement: analysis and optimization of application performance (legal basis: legitimate interest)
  • User Support: management of support and assistance requests (legal basis: contract execution)
  • Security: prevention of fraud, abuse, and illegal activities (legal basis: legitimate interest)

4. Data Retention Period

Personal data is retained for the time strictly necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Account data: retained until account deletion by the user
  • Support data: retained for 2 years from the last interaction

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes. Data may be shared only with:

  • Service providers: companies that provide technical services on our behalf (e.g., hosting, analytics)
  • Legal obligations: when required by law or to protect our rights

6. User Rights

In accordance with GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: obtain confirmation of whether your data is being processed and access to it
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data
  • Deletion: request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction: request limitation of data processing
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Objection: object to data processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise your rights, contact us at: privacy@riseagainsthunger.it

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the application or via email.

9. Contact

For questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@riseagainsthunger.it
  • Website: italy.riseagainsthunger.org
  • Address: Rise Against Hunger Italia, Italy
