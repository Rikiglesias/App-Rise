Delete your account in the «RAH Italia» app

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This page is about the RAH Italia app, listed on Google Play as «Rise Against Hunger Italia», by the developer Rise Against Hunger Italia. It explains how to delete your account, which data we delete, which data we keep and for how long.

1. In the app

  1. Open the app and tap the fourth tab, Profile, in the bottom bar.
  2. If you are not already signed in, sign in with your email and password.
  3. In the Privacy and data section, tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete now and confirm with Yes, proceed.

Your account and the data listed in section 3 are deleted immediately, with no waiting period.

2. Without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, write to info@riseagainsthunger.it with the subject «Delete my RAH Italia app account». To make sure nobody deletes someone else's account, we will ask you to confirm the request by replying from the email address you signed up with. We delete the account within one month of your confirmation and let you know by email.

3. What we delete

  • your sign-in account: email address and password;
  • your profile: first name, last name, nickname, phone, city, province, country, date of birth, contact email, your choice about our communications and the date you accepted the privacy notice;
  • the record of the consents you gave in the app;
  • the link between your account and the referral code the app adds to links to our partners Donorbox and Let's Donation;
  • if our historical contact archive had a record with the same email, and you confirmed that email when signing up, that record as well.

4. What we keep and for how long

  • The referral code, without your name or email: a random number we use to ask Donorbox and Let's Donation to delete anything they linked to it. On its own it cannot identify you; it currently has no automatic expiry.
  • Technical logs of the provider that hosts the app (Supabase, servers in the European Union): IP address, date and time of requests. They are deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • Problem reports you chose to send us by email: they stay in the support mailbox info@riseagainsthunger.it (Google Workspace) even after the account is deleted, because they are not linked to your app profile. They currently have no automatic expiry: you can ask us to delete them by writing to us; details are in the privacy policy.

5. Donations and partner websites

Donations are not linked to your app account: they are made on the Donorbox website. Events, projects and the shop are on the Let's Donation website, which has its own account. Data you left on those websites is not deleted when you delete your app account: to delete it, write to them, or to us and we will forward your request.

6. Timing and questions

In the app, deletion is immediate; by email, within one month of your confirmation. For any question: info@riseagainsthunger.it. The full privacy notice is in the app privacy policy.
